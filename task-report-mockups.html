Create task

Set up the task step by step. Each section explains how its choices affect who can report work and what happens after submission.

Required Must be filled in. Optional Can be left blank. Conditional Required only when the related option is enabled.

1. Task details

Name the work and explain what a member should do. Choose the department that owns this task; this affects department permissions and reporting.

Shown to members when they report completion.
Default 1 PAP per completion. Tasks under 2 hours are typically 1 PAP; add 1 PAP for each additional 2-hour block. Pilots do not enter time, and this is not calculated automatically.
Your department choices are limited to groups where you have task-creation permission. Admins with global permission can create tasks for any department.

2. Who can complete this task?

Anyone in a selected group or any selected individual can submit. Choose at least one group or individual.

Group and individual assignments are combined.

3. Completion approval

Choose whether submissions are accepted right away or held for review by selected approvers.

Required when approval is enabled. Any member of a selected group can review.

4. Repeat submissions and limits

Decide whether the task closes after one accepted report or stays available. Optional caps are lifetime totals and do not reset on a schedule.

Leave blank for no cap. Pending, returned-for-revision and approved reports count; denied reports do not.
Only applies if a completion limit is set.
